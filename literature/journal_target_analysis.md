# Journal target analysis

Updated: 2026-08-21

## Primary: Journal of Systems Architecture

The official scope covers embedded software design from system software to
application-specific architecture, explicitly including mobile systems,
real-time systems, parallel architectures, and software-oriented
hardware/software co-design. The narrowed paper is fundamentally a mobile
runtime and measurement-methodology study, making this the best thematic fit.

The journal warns that case studies must contribute more than a design for one
piece of software. Submission therefore requires a generalizable protocol,
statistical rank-stability analysis, reusable benchmark implementation, and
open raw evidence—not only results from one application.

The publisher page currently lists a USD 2,450 open-access charge and a
subscription route with no stated publication fee. Article requirements still
need to be captured from the live guide immediately before submission.

## Secondary: Internet of Things

The official scope includes Artificial Intelligence of Things and Intelligent
Edge work. It supports full research papers, open software/data, tutorials and
best practices, and case studies. The fit becomes credible if the paper frames
the phone as an intelligent edge node and emphasizes reproducible deployment
selection rather than generic image classification.

The official page currently lists a USD 3,020 open-access charge and a
subscription route with no stated publication fee. The paper should not be
artificially redesigned around IoT terminology merely to fit this venue.

## Backup: Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence

The official guide accepts benchmarking exercises and software/hardware
architectures for real-time AI, but it also requires a novel AI aspect and
real-world engineering validation on public datasets. A careful replication
and benchmark protocol may be judged insufficient without a stronger selection
method or broader device evidence. It is therefore a backup, not the target
driving the research design.

The guide specifies a single-column submission, double-anonymized review, a
maximum 250-word abstract, one to six keywords, and a required research-data
availability route (deposit/link or explain why unavailable).

## SCIE verification status

No journal is labelled SCIE in project outputs yet. Clarivate's Master Journal
List is the authoritative target, but direct journal-profile confirmation was
not available from the search interface during this audit. Publisher impact
factors and third-party index lists are not treated as substitutes for a
current SCIE profile. Before target finalization, open the Master Journal List,
search each ISSN, save the dated profile evidence, and update
`journal_targets.csv`.
