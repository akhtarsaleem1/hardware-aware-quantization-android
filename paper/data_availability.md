# Data availability

DeepWeeds is available under CC BY 4.0 from its official repository. The submission artifact contains the frozen capture-session-grouped manifests and hashes, training-only calibration indices, validation/parity reports, the balanced protocol-1.2 trials 1-2 Android CSV (SHA-256 `5c129b5d6adfa10e3db3f4f66e118665470e89ceffd780d414dcc06483dd762b`) plus immutable provenance for the incomplete source run, the locked-test per-sample prediction CSV and aggregate JSON, derived tables, and publication figures. Source images are not duplicated beyond what the DeepWeeds license and submission venue permit. A Zenodo record has been reserved for the reproducibility artifact at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22082237 and will be made public before manuscript submission.
