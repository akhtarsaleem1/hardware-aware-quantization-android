# Data availability

DeepWeeds is available under CC BY 4.0 from its official repository. The submission artifact contains the frozen capture-session-grouped manifests and hashes, training-only calibration indices, validation/parity reports, the balanced protocol-1.2 trials 1-2 Android CSV (SHA-256 `5c129b5d6adfa10e3db3f4f66e118665470e89ceffd780d414dcc06483dd762b`) plus immutable provenance for the incomplete source run, the locked-test per-sample prediction CSV and aggregate JSON, derived tables, and publication figures. Source images are not duplicated beyond what the DeepWeeds license and submission venue permit. The v1.0.0 reproducibility artifact is publicly available on Zenodo at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22082237.
